# Deep Time — Wayfinder Map

Labels: `wayfinder:map`

## Destination

A locked design + build spec at `.claude/plans/deep-time-spec.md` for a scroll-driven site telling the 4.6-billion-year history of Earth — complete enough that a fresh implementation session builds it without making a single further decision.

Not the built site. The map ends at handoff.

## Notes

**Domain:** a dustincoledata data-toy. New repo `Projects/Deep_Time`, Astro, ships to its own subdomain with a card on dustincoledata.com/projects (both post-handoff, out of scope here).

**Skills every session should consult:** `/impeccable` (visual craft), `/intent:*` (UX), `/prototype` (cheap artifacts to react to), `/grilling` + `/domain-modeling` (default), `/dataviz` if any chart appears.

**Hard constraints:**

- Earth is **4.6 billion** years old. The original brief said 46 billion — wrong, corrected.
- **Scientific accuracy is non-negotiable.** A site whose entire claim is "true to scale" cannot fudge a date or a scale. Every milestone needs an authoritative source.
- **Size:** ~3–5 minute single-sitting scroll. ~25–40 milestones, ~10–15 environment visuals. Shareable and finishable; the payoff must land while attention holds.
- **Mobile is first-class.** Scroll-driven sites get opened on phones. WebGL degrades gracefully; a real-device phone test is a ship gate. Prior scars: iOS first-tap-is-hover, iOS URL-bar canvas squash.
- **Nothing overlaps, anywhere, ever.** Dustin, explicit: *"stuff can't overlap. spacing must be good throughout site with text and elements etc."* Site-wide, text and elements alike. The mechanism is the **no-collision layout contract** in [Environment cadence](issues/06-environment-cadence.md): reserved zones for the clock and the scale bar, a non-overlapping slot grid for everything else, each arrival a single box holding its own art and text, and all travel confined inside that box. It is verifiable — sweep the scroll and assert zero rectangle intersections — and that check is a **ship gate**, not a review note.
- **Art spend:** gpt-image-1 approved; the style gate is now lifted — [Visual identity & art direction](issues/03-visual-identity.md) locked the recipe. Standing rule: **proof first, Dustin approves before any batch.** No exploratory burn on throwaway looks.
- **Anchor before mocks:** lock a loved reference + honest positioning before building any mock.
- **Borrowed by observation, not by asset:** the anchor's look was read off the live site and re-encoded. Nothing from neal.fun ships.
- **Plan, don't do.** Tickets resolve decisions. The only "doing" allowed is cheap prototypes and proof artifacts that exist to settle a decision.

**The payoff:** the whole experience exists to deliver one moment — the human era revealed as a vanishingly small sliver at the end. Every decision is judged by whether it makes that moment hit harder.

## Decisions so far

<!-- one line per closed ticket: gist + link -->

- [Scroll & scale mechanic](issues/01-scroll-scale-mechanic.md) — pure linear, **1 px = 40,000 years, never rescaled**; 123,600 px total; the scale is explained up front, a true-scale bar sits on the right the whole way, and the ten moments too recent to draw are withheld from the scroll and delivered at the end as a labelled fan converging on one pixel.
- [Visual identity & art direction](issues/03-visual-identity.md) — **[neal.fun/deep-sea](https://neal.fun/deep-sea/) done for deep time**: ~30 painted cut-out **subjects on a code-drawn field** (not full-screen environments), generated by gpt-image-1 in **sheets of 4** in a locked bright natural-history-plate style, each verified against a real reference; background colour is a **data channel** (the era's true sky and ocean, with the Great Oxidation and Snowball as the two earned events); type is **Archivo only**, tabular figures. Scale carries the payoff, art carries the 88%.
- [Environment cadence across the Precambrian](issues/06-environment-cadence.md) — **42 arrivals over 101,530 px in three weights** (19 milestone · 18 inhabitant/condition · 5 field event), where recurrence of persistent conditions, not new art, buys the rate; **the Boring Billion is named and deliberately left empty** (a held plate + a pixels-to-go counter + a slowed field) rather than filled with repetition; **every field transition takes its true duration** — the Great Oxidation is 9,750 px, the whole Cryogenian only ~2,125 px; the **receding Moon** (2.5× → 1.06×) is promoted to the slow clock that carries dead air. Ships with the **no-collision layout contract**.
- [Milestone set & verified dates](issues/02-milestone-set.md) — **54 verified arrivals (30 milestone · 19 inhabitant · 5 field) with zero gaps under the 600 px readability floor**, so the finale fan is **30 + 10**, exactly as 01 assumed. The floor (**600 px = 24 million years**) is what does the editing: it cuts *T. rex*, the first primates and *Dickinsonia* on arithmetic alone, and it is what stops the recent end being crowded. Every contested date is flagged and **its hedge is now a hard requirement on the card copy**. Three corrections handed back to closed tickets — the **Great Oxidation seam is 5,250 px, not 9,750**; the **Marinoan is ~100 px, not 375**; and the Archean orange haze may be dated ~1.1 Ga too early. Art order drops to **~13 generations** (~35 distinct subjects in 9 sheets + the same 4 full-bleeds), because the withheld ten are label-only.

## Not yet specified

- **Copy & narration** — voice, and whether there's a narrator at all or only labels + dates. **Unblocked** — [02](issues/02-milestone-set.md) delivered the set, and with it one hard requirement: every ⚠ date carries its hedge *in the card*, not in a footer. Density is now settled by the mechanic, the art direction and the layout contract: cards carry date + name + **one line on desktop, no line on mobile** (a phone band cannot hold art + name + a line, and losing the art costs more than losing the line), never paragraphs; the ten withheld moments are label-only.
- **Audio** — whether the experience has sound (ambience per era, a tone at the reveal) or is silent. Not yet clear it earns its cost.
- **Site name + domain** — "Deep Time" is a working folder name, not a decided brand. The public name and subdomain are open.
- **Final spec assembly** — the shape of the handoff document itself, once the decisions above exist to write down.

## Out of scope

- **Building and shipping the site.** The destination is the spec; implementation is a separate effort.
- **Universe-scale (13.8B) or future/forward timelines.** This map is Earth, past to present.
- **The dustincoledata.com/projects brand card and subdomain setup.** Post-build.

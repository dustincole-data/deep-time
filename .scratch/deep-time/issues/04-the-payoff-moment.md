## Question

Exactly how does the human-era reveal land?

The entire site exists for this one moment. "Humans are a tiny sliver at the end" is the *idea*; this ticket decides the *staging* — what the visitor sees, when, and what makes them sit back.

Open:

- Where the reveal sits: at the end of a continuous scroll, or a deliberate act break where the rules of the page change.
- The mechanic of the punch — zoom out, rewind, a comparison mark, a counter, the scroll suddenly refusing to move, something else entirely.
- What the visitor is left holding: a number, an image, a sentence, silence.
- Whether it is **repeatable** (worth scrolling again, worth showing someone) or a one-shot trick that dies on the second view.
- The share artifact — what a person posts after they feel it, and whether that's an OG image, a stat, or a link with a fragment.

Blocked by [Scroll & scale mechanic](01-scroll-scale-mechanic.md): the reveal's staging depends entirely on how scroll maps to time.

**Deliverable:** a rough prototype of the reveal, plus the staging specified beat by beat.
